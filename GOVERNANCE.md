# Governance

Nathan Mulligan is the maintainer. He decides product direction, merges,
releases, and repository policy. Builders do not approve their own work.
[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) defines the required model reviews, seven
merge questions, and the conformance proof required for each of the eight
protected components.

## Continuity

There is one maintainer and no demonstrated independent restore capability.
No successor is appointed by this document. An operator designation alone does
not prove that another person can recover an installation.

Before a release, a second operator must complete installation, restore, and
partial-migration recovery from the written runbook without the author helping.
No recovery figures are published until measured in that rehearsal. The
installer and recovery procedure are not built.

The AGPL licence permits continued development through a fork under its terms.

## Decisions and maintainership

[Current decisions](docs/current-decisions.md) records the public technical
baseline. ADRs explain durable trade-offs. Tickets carry implementation scope,
acceptance criteria, and review evidence. The glossary defines domain terms.

Outside contributions are closed. If they open, maintainership requires a
record of sound implementation and review, responsibility for an area, and a
written invitation and acceptance. No contribution count automatically grants
maintainer authority.

The maintainer changes this policy. Any later shared governance arrangement
must be recorded explicitly.
