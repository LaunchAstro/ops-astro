# Contributor licence agreement

**Draft. Subject to counsel, and counsel is deferred.** This text has not
been reviewed by a lawyer, and legal review remains outstanding. See
`LICENSING.md`.
It is retained so contributors can inspect the proposed terms before
contributions open.

**Nobody is being asked to sign anything today.** Contributions are closed.
The signing tool goes in before the first outside contribution is merged.

## The parties

- **Nathan Mulligan**, referred to as "the Maintainer".

The recorded position on 8 September 2026 is that no Launch Astro legal

## What this is, in one line

You keep your copyright. You give the Maintainer permission to use your
contribution, including under a different licence.

## What this is not

- **Not an assignment.** Your copyright stays yours. You may use your own
  contribution anywhere else, for anything, without asking.
- **Not exclusive.** You may license the same work to anyone else.
- **Not a replacement for the DCO.** You still sign off every commit. The
  sign-off says you may give it; this says what the Maintainer may do with it.

## The grant

1. **Copyright.** You grant the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to reproduce, prepare
   derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and
   distribute your contribution and the derivative works of it.

2. **Relicensing.** That licence includes the right to distribute your
   contribution under terms other than the AGPL, including a commercial
   licence. This is the clause the whole agreement exists for. The public
   project stays AGPL-3.0-only; this permission is what makes it possible to
   also offer the same code to a buyer whose policy forbids AGPL software.

3. **Patents.** You grant the Maintainer and every recipient of the software
   a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent
   licence to make, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer
   your contribution, limited to the patent claims you own or control that
   are necessarily infringed by your contribution alone or by its combination
   with the project.

4. **Defensive termination.** If You begin patent litigation against anyone,
   by claim or by counterclaim, alleging that this project or a contribution
   in it infringes a patent, then **every patent licence this project has
   granted to You terminates** on the day the proceeding is filed. The
   licences You granted under clause 3 are unaffected and continue.

   This follows the pattern in section 3 of the Apache License 2.0. It points
   the way the earlier draft did not: an earlier version of this file
   terminated the licence **You granted**, which would have withdrawn the
   project's own protection at the exact moment it was needed. The sweep of
   6 September caught it. A defensive clause has to disarm the party who
   sued, not the party who was sued.

   Worked example, which is the test any replacement wording must pass. A
   contributor's employer sues a customer of this project, alleging the
   project infringes its patent. Under this clause the employer's group loses
   the patent licence it received and the customer keeps the licence the
   contributor granted. Under the earlier wording the customer would have
   lost their protection instead, which is the opposite of the point.

## What You confirm

- The contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it
  under this agreement and have said so.
- If your employer has rights in work you produce, either they have waived
  them for this contribution or you have their permission to make it.
- Every third party's material in your contribution is identified, with its
  licence, and you have the right to include it.
- You are not knowingly including anyone's confidential information,
  personal information or trade secrets.

## What You do not promise

Your contribution is provided as is. You give no warranty of any kind, except
that You may lawfully make the grant above. Nothing in this agreement
excludes a right that cannot be excluded under Australian law.

## Signing

When contributions open, a bot comments on your first pull request with a
link. You confirm once and it remembers. There is no paperwork to post and
nothing to scan.

## Governing law

The law of Queensland, Australia. This clause is one of the several in this
draft that counsel will look at first.

## For counsel

Open points, listed so they are not lost:

- Whether relicensing needs the sublicensing right stated separately from the
  distribution right.
- Whether the defensive termination in clause 4 should reach a counterclaim
  raised in defence of an unrelated action, which the Apache wording does not
  distinguish.
- Whether the corporate contributor case needs its own document.
- Whether the Australian Consumer Law reaches a no-fee contribution, and what
  survives of the as-is clause if it does.
- How the assignment to the entity, once formed, is effected for agreements
  signed before it existed.
