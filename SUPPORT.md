# Support

There is no general support service, service-level agreement, response-time
guarantee, or commitment to future features. The foundation has no application,
release, or installer to support.

Security reports follow [Security](SECURITY.md), including its acknowledgement
and disclosure policy. It names no private reporting route, because none has
been proved. Code of conduct reports follow
[the code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Issues are open, as information. Report a bug or describe a problem and it
will be read. An open issue is a record, not a commitment to act on it, and
it creates no support guarantee. Unsolicited pull requests are closed:
[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains why and what to open instead.

No recovery-time or recovery-point figures are published until an independent
restore has been rehearsed and measured.
