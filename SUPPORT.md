# Support

There is no general support service, service-level agreement, response-time
guarantee, or commitment to future features. The foundation has no application,
release, or installer to support.

Security reports follow [Security](SECURITY.md), including its acknowledgement
and disclosure policy. Mailbox delivery remains unverified. Code of conduct
reports follow [the code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Outside issues and contributions remain closed. If those channels open,
bugs and proposals belong in the repository's issue tracker. Opening a channel
does not create a support guarantee.

No recovery-time or recovery-point figures are published until an independent
restore has been rehearsed and measured.
