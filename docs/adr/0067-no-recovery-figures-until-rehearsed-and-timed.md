# Publish recovery figures only after measured rehearsal

Accepted 6 September 2026; supersedes earlier estimated recovery targets.

Publish no recovery-point or recovery-time figure until a restore has been
rehearsed and measured for the named installation profile. Treat the two
measurements separately. An estimate, backup setting, or designated operator
is not recovery proof.

Before a release, an independent operator must install, restore, and recover
from a partial migration using the runbook without help from its author.
Record the tested versions, data, failure, elapsed time, and evidence of the
restored result. No such product rehearsal is established by this foundation.
[Governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md) keeps continuity status explicit.
